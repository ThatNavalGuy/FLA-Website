What is FLA?

We run organized roleplay sessions in ER:LC set in Florida. Our staff enforce the same rules for everyone so every session stays fair and fun.

Staff

A trained staff team

Staff follow set procedures and policies, enforce the regulations fairly and help with questions or reports. Ask in the Discord.
