What is FLA?

Florida State Roleplay is a Florida-themed roleplay community for Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) on Roblox. We run organized roleplay sessions where you can serve as a trooper, respond as a firefighter, or build your own criminal story. Our staff keep every session fair so everyone can enjoy it.

How to join

  1. Join our Discord. It has announcements, rules and support.
  2. Join the Roblox group.
  3. Read the regulations in the Discord.
  4. Join in-game and start roleplaying.

Staff

Our staff team is trained and follows set procedures and policies. They enforce the regulations the same way for everyone and help players with questions or reports. Need help? Open the Discord and ask.

Whitelisted

FLA Whitelisted is our approved-members side of the community. It offers a higher standard of roleplay and access to our whitelisted servers. Join the Whitelisted Discord or the Whitelisted group to get started.

Departments

We have two departments you can apply to:

  • Miami Law Enforcement: patrol, respond to calls and enforce the law.
  • Miami Fire Rescue: respond to fires and medical emergencies.

Ownership

FLA is led by a great ownership team. They work on the community every day, listen to feedback and hold everyone to the same standard. The quality of FLA comes from their work.
