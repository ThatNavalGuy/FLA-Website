What is FLA?

Roleplay set in the Sunshine State

We run organized roleplay sessions in ER:LC where players act out life in Florida. You can serve as a trooper, respond as a firefighter, or build your own criminal story. Our staff keep every session fair, so everyone can enjoy it.

How it works

Getting started in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Join our Discord

    This is our main hub. You’ll find announcements, rules, support and session info here.

  2. 2

    Join the Roblox group

    The group is how we recognize our members in game.

  3. 3

    Read the regulations

    Our rules keep roleplay realistic and fair. Read them before your first session.

  4. 4

    Roleplay

    Join a session as a civilian, or apply for a department or the whitelist for more.

Staff

Moderated by a trained team

Our staff team makes sure FLA stays fair and active.

  • Enforce the rules. They apply our regulations the same way to everyone.
  • Help players. Ask questions or report issues in our Discord and staff will assist.
  • Follow clear standards. Every staff member is trained and follows set procedures and policies.

Ownership

A great ownership team

FLA is led by an ownership team that cares about this community. They build and improve the server every day, listen to feedback and hold everyone to the same high standard, including themselves. The quality of FLA comes from their work.
