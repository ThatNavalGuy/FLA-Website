WELCOME TO FLA

Good roleplay is built by the people in it.

Florida State Roleplay is an ER:LC community for players who want more from a session than simply joining a server and driving around. The best scenes come from people communicating, taking their roles seriously, and giving others something to play off.

Whether you're interested in public safety, civilian roleplay, or helping behind the scenes, there's room to get involved. You don't need to know everything on day one. Show up, learn the expectations, and build from there.

01 / PLAY
⌁

Make scenes worth remembering

Work with other players to create believable traffic stops, emergency calls, investigations, and everyday Florida stories.

02 / LEARN
↗

Build skills as you go

Practice communication, teamwork, decision-making, and understanding procedures in a roleplay setting.

03 / CONTRIBUTE
✳

Help shape the community

Give constructive feedback, support new members, and take on more responsibility when opportunities open.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

More ways to get involved

Whether you want to play, take on a specialist role, or help run the community, there are different ways to contribute.

◎

FOR PLAYERS

Whitelisted Roles

Whitelisted roles can offer a more structured roleplay experience, with specific responsibilities, expectations, and sometimes additional training.

Take time to understand the role, read the requirements, and show that you can communicate clearly, follow procedures, and work well with others.

Applications may require activity, maturity, knowledge of the rules, or an interview. Check the current requirements before applying.

Application information →
✳

FOR COMMUNITY BUILDERS

Join the Staff Team

Staff help create the conditions for good roleplay. Depending on the position, that can mean answering questions, helping members understand rules, reviewing reports, or supporting community events.

It's an opportunity to build leadership, teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills while helping the community improve.

Staff is a responsibility, not just a title. Fairness, patience, activity, professionalism, and accountability matter.

Why staff matters →
↗

FOR CREATIVE PLAYERS

Events & Projects

Get involved in organised sessions, suggest scenario ideas, help welcome new members, or contribute to community projects when opportunities are announced.

Events can give players a reason to work together, try different roles, and create memorable scenes.

Share ideas constructively, listen to feedback, and work with others to turn good suggestions into well-organised sessions.

BE PART OF THE TEAM

Good staff make a good community possible.

Being staff means helping people enjoy the game fairly. It takes patience, consistency, and a willingness to handle situations calmly, even when things get difficult.

Ask About Staff Openings →
01

Support Players

Help members find information, understand expectations, and work through issues respectfully.

02

Keep Things Fair

Apply rules consistently. Good moderation is calm, clear, impartial, and accountable.

03

Help FLA Grow

Support events, share useful feedback, and help make new and returning players feel welcome.

04

Build Responsibility
